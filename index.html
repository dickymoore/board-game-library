About the ratings, editions and remaining mysteries

Ratings are BGG community scores out of 10, checked against publicly indexed sources on 13–14 October 2026. Source pages may have been crawled earlier and scores can change. “Not verified” means a dependable score or exact game match could not be established. It does not mean a score of zero.

The review on each card is a short, original paraphrase of a named BGG community review or comment, linked to its source. These are individual views, not a BGG editorial verdict. Where a review could not be verified, that is stated. Categories are practical catalogue labels and may combine game type, theme and mechanism. Player counts and times describe the matched game; the edition note explains any uncertainty.

Separate owned editions remain separate entries. A shared rating means the underlying game was matched, not that every physical edition has a separate BGG listing. Public images may show a different printing of the same game. Original household photographs, private notes and local file paths are not included in the shareable files.
