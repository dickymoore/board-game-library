# Dicky's Games & Books Library

A searchable household catalogue at https://dickymoore.github.io/board-game-library/ . Switch between **Books** and **Games** from the front page.

The book catalogue contains 798 entries and 810 reconciled copy records from 82 unique photographs supplied on 22 October 2026. Locations were confirmed by the owner. 729 entries are identified, 28 need a title or author check, and 41 are explicit unreadable or hidden-spine records. Utility-room recipe books are deferred. The inventory does not establish the contents of unseen shelves.

Search books by title or author, filter by location/category, show books being read or needing a closer look, and sort by title, author, location or Goodreads rating. Print the active selection to PDF or export it as CSV. `books.json` is the public data export. Copies in different places and genuine duplicates retain their locations.

599 book entries have a Goodreads score with a source and check date. Missing scores remain unavailable and are never treated as zero. Public artwork is representative and may show a different printing; missing images use a clearly marked placeholder. Cover and rating source links are on the cards. Goodreads community scores are dated snapshots, not ratings by the owner.

The Games view preserves the original 77 game entries (73 main entries and four expansions/add-ons), filters, cover pictures, BoardGameGeek ratings, attributed community-review summaries and exports. `games.html` opens that browser directly; `library.html` and `library.pdf` are the original standalone game exports. Game research dates remain 13–14 October 2026.

This is a buildless static site with no accounts, analytics or server-side storage. Some reference covers may load from their public source hosts. Cover artwork belongs to its respective rights holders. No affiliation with Goodreads, BoardGameGeek or publishers is implied.

Household photographs, OCR, private notes and identification evidence are deliberately excluded. Editable sources and the private identification review remain in the owner's local vault.
